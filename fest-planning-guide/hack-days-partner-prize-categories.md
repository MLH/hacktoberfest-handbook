# Hack Days Partner Prize Categories

{% hint style="info" %}
**Coming soon:** Full partner challenge copy, participant resources, opening slides, and prize details will be published after partner approval.
{% endhint %}

Some `Hacktoberfest Hack Day` events will receive an additional Hack Days partner prize category. The MLH Hacktoberfest team assigns these categories to selected events and adds them to OrganizerHQ automatically.

Until the full pages are published, use the partner information in your event onboarding. If an assigned category is missing from OrganizerHQ, contact [hacktoberfest@mlh.io](mailto:hacktoberfest@mlh.io). Do not create a replacement.
